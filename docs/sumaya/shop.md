# Shop and gamepasses

Sumaya has two separate "shops". This page covers both:

1. The **Main Menu Shop** tab, where you spend **Robux** on gamepasses and cash packs.
2. **In-world shops**, where you spend **Ringgit** on food, gas cans, and tools at stalls around town.

Both are open to every player.

## The Main Menu Shop tab

Open the main menu (either from the join screen or via the **Open Menu** house icon in the top bar), then tap the **Shop** tab. You'll see a grid of cards: gamepasses on top and one-time cash purchases below.

Each card shows a title, a short description, and either a **Purchase** button or a **Coming Soon** label. Coming Soon cards preview content the team is still building out: the picture, name, and description are visible, but purchase is disabled until the item is ready.

### What's purchasable right now

| Item | Type | What you get |
|------|------|--------------|

Non-owners can see the cards locked behind a gamepass prompt. See [Vehicles](/sumaya/vehicles). |

### How gamepasses persist

- Gamepass ownership is tied to your **Roblox account**, so it follows you across every Sumaya server and every play session.
- One-time cash purchases drop straight into your **bank account** with a paycheck-style notification. They're tracked per-receipt so a duplicate purchase notification cannot double-pay you.
- Owning a vehicle gamepass unlocks the relevant vehicles at the normal dealer; you still pay the in-game **Ringgit** cost (most are not free).
- If you owned a legacy vehicle pack (Premium, Supercar, EV, KEMRonda, Traffic POLIS, Unmarked POLIS, Classic / Retro) before the Coming Soon cards went live, that ownership still counts; you don't need to repurchase.

### Refunds and disputes

In-game gamepass / cash purchases go through Roblox's normal Marketplace, so refunds and payment disputes are handled by Roblox support, not the Sumaya team.

## In-world shops (Ringgit)

In-world shops let you spend Ringgit on consumables, tools, and roleplay items. They're scattered around the map and marked on the minimap.

### Food stalls

Cheap food items, currently cosmetic / roleplay flavour. They don't actually restore hunger in the current build.

- **Mamak Stall**: drinks like Kopi Tenom and Teh Tarik, plus simple meals.
- **Nasi Lemak Stall**: a small menu of Malaysian classics (Nasi Lemak, Nasi Goreng, Roti Canai, Milo Ais).

Prices range from 1 to 5 SR per item.

### Boat gas can shop

Sells refuel cans for fishing trawlers. Cars do not need refuelling.

| Can | Refill | Price |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Red | +25 fuel | 75 SR |
| Green | +50 fuel | 100 SR |
| Black | +75 fuel | 125 SR |

Trawler fuel caps at 100 and **refill only works when current fuel is below 75**, so a top-up on an almost-full tank does nothing and won't consume the can.

### Tools and consumables

Crime tools (Slim Jim, Pickpocket Knife) are handed out for free by the **Abang Samseng** NPCs rather than sold at shops. See [Crime and Law](/sumaya/crime-and-law).

## Tips

- **Browse the shop tab on every visit.** Coming Soon cards turn into Purchase cards quietly once the team finishes wiring them up; there's no in-game notification.
- **The Retro Vehicles pack is the only paid pack you can buy right now.** Until the Premium EV / Sport / Luxury packs flip live, the standard civilian dealer is your only source of paid vehicles.
- **Cash packs are bank-to-bank.** They land in your bank, so the 500 SR wallet cap doesn't matter. You can save up for a big purchase by stacking cash packs straight into bank.
- **In-world stall food is cosmetic for now.** Don't expect Teh Tarik to restore stamina; the hunger / stamina HUD isn't wired up in the current build.
- **Gas cans only top up boats.** Cars run without fuel limits.
