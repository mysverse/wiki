# Vehicles

Bandaraya has a large vehicle roster: civilian cars, motorcycles, buses, tow trucks, police vehicles, fire appliances, military vehicles, and helicopters.

## TL;DR

- Use the spawner your team is allowed to use.
- Civilians use the **Classic Car Dealer** for normal vehicles.
- Fuel matters.
- Crashes damage vehicles.
- Bought dealership vehicles are saved to your account.

## Spawning a vehicle

1. Walk to a vehicle spawner or the Classic Car Dealer.
2. Interact with the prompt.
3. Browse the available vehicles.
4. Spawn the one you want.
5. Sit in the driver seat.

If a spawner refuses access, you likely do not qualify for that team or vehicle.

## Driving

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **W / S** | Accelerate / brake |
| **A / D** | Steer |
| **Space** | Handbrake on many vehicles |
| **F1** | Mouse steering |
| **C / V** | Camera controls on some vehicles |
| On-screen buttons | Horns, sirens, doors, lights, and other vehicle-specific controls |

Mouse steering gives finer control than keyboard-only steering.

## Fuel

| Fuel type | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **Petrol / diesel** | Radtrol Gas Station at Jalan Pinang |
| **Electric** | Community Retail charging stations |

To refuel, drive to the pump or charger, exit, interact, pay BR, and wait for the fuel bar.

## Crash damage

Vehicles can be damaged by:

- High-speed crashes.
- Vehicle-to-vehicle impacts.
- PIT-style collisions.
- Heavy contact with walls or objects.

Drive carefully. Deliberate vehicle griefing can lead to moderation action.

## Registration and plates

Spawned vehicles receive plates automatically. Police and tablet users with access can use plate lookup for traffic stops, tow work, and vehicle management.

## Armoured vehicles

Some vehicles protect occupants better than normal cars:

- POLIS AV4
- POLIS V150
- UTK Hilux variants

Civilian cars are not bulletproof.

## Dealership: Classic Car Dealer

The Classic Car Dealer sells civilian vehicles.

### Common brands

- Proton
- Perodua
- Toyota
- Honda
- Ford
- BMW
- Mitsubishi

### Options

- **Buy:** permanent account-owned vehicle.
- **Rent:** temporary vehicle.
- **Paint:** colour customisation.

HCC players also get access to luxury vehicles such as BMW i7, Volvo XC90, and Mercedes C300.

## Helicopters

POLIS and BOMBA have team helicopter spawners. Civilians cannot spawn emergency helicopters.

## Tips

- Public-service vehicles are team-gated.
- Some vehicles have more controls than others.
- Motorcycles now park more reliably after dismounting.
- Respawn a damaged or lost vehicle from the correct spawner.
- Do not use vehicles to spawn-ram, block scenes, or harass players.
